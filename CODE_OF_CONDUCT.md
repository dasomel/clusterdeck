# Code of Conduct

ClusterDeck is committed to a respectful and constructive open-source community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus criticism on ideas, designs, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Keep security reports private until coordinated disclosure is possible.
- Help keep discussions useful, reproducible, and accessible to future contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, malicious disclosure of private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainer privately for conduct concerns or use the project's private GitHub reporting mechanisms when available.
